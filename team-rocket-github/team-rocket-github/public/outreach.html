01Python programming camp

A first line of code. A whole new world.

Project VisionChinatown, Chicago

We partnered with Project Vision to lead a six-week Python camp for students from lower-income families in Chicago’s Chinatown. The goal was simple: make the chance to explore coding more accessible.

Six weeks of introducing students to programming, led by the people who know what it’s like to be learning it themselves.

DECODE kickoffWheaton Academy

At the 2025–26 DECODE kickoff at Wheaton Academy, we ran a CAD workshop to share design skills with the robotics community.
