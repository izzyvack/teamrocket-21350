We are Team Rocket, a community based FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) team based in Naperville, Illinois. Our team is made up of 8 members ranging from middle school to high school and all levels of experience. We have competed in FTC since 2022, and this will be our fifth year competing.

Our Mission

To bring together students from schools across Chicagoland and inspire the next generation of engineers by creating opportunities for young students interested in STEM. We want students of all backgrounds, regardless of gender or economic status, to have access to an encouraging environment where they can explore engineering, collaborate with others, and develop their ideas.
