About us

Who We Are

Our team brings together students from schools across the Naperville area. We work together on the mechanical design, programming, and outreach that make up our FTC season.

Our Mission

Our goal is to encourage more girls to pursue engineering and make STEM education accessible to students in our community. Through robotics and outreach, we provide opportunities to learn programming, design, and teamwork.
