A TEAM
OF OUR OWN.

An all-girls, community-based FIRST Tech Challenge team from Naperville, Illinois. We design robots, chase better ideas, and make room for the next generation of women in STEM.

Hardware, CAD, software, and outreach all bring something different to the table. What connects us is a willingness to learn from each other.
