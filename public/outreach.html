Outreach

We work with schools, community organizations, and other robotics teams to make STEM education more accessible.

Project Vision

Python Programming Camp

Chinatown, Chicago

Six-week program

We partnered with Project Vision to teach a six-week Python programming camp for students from lower-income families in Chicago’s Chinatown.

Team members introduced students to programming and helped make coding education more accessible in the community.

DECODE kickoff

CAD Workshop

Wheaton Academy

2025–26 season kickoff

At the 2025–26 DECODE kickoff at Wheaton Academy, we ran a CAD workshop to share design skills with the robotics community.

The workshop gave us an opportunity to introduce other students to computer-aided design and share what we have learned through FTC.
